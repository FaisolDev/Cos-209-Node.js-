EduMate – Product Requirements Document (PRD)

1. Project Overview

Project Name: EduMate

Project Type: Educational Support Platform

Target Institution: Babcock University

EduMate is a platform designed to help students connect with other students for academic support, group studying and skill sharing.

The idea came from the difficulty students sometimes experience when trying to find people to study with or get help with certain courses. EduMate aims to make this process easier by providing a common place where students can connect based on their courses and interests.

2. Problem Statement

University students sometimes struggle to find suitable study partners or people who can help them understand difficult topics.

For example, a student may have difficulty understanding a particular course but may not know another student who understands it well. Some students also want to study in groups but find it difficult to organize one with people taking the same courses.

Although students use WhatsApp groups, friends and other informal methods to get academic assistance, these options do not always make it easy to find the right people.

EduMate aims to address this problem by creating a platform where students can find study partners, create study groups and exchange skills.

3. Background and Research

The project focuses on academic collaboration among students of Babcock University.

Some of the situations the project intends to investigate include:

- Students having difficulty finding study partners.
- Students relying on friends and classmates for academic assistance.
- Difficulty organizing study groups.
- Students having skills they could teach others but lacking a simple way to connect with interested learners.

Research still to be completed:

The team should interview students from different departments and levels to understand how they currently find study partners and what challenges they experience.

The findings will be added here after the interviews.

4. Target Users

The main users of EduMate are Babcock University students.

They include:

1. Students looking for people to study with.
2. Students who need help understanding particular courses.
3. Students interested in teaching other students.
4. Students who want to learn new skills from their colleagues.

5. Existing Solutions

Students currently use different methods to connect with one another academically.

WhatsApp Groups: Students communicate, share materials and ask questions in class or departmental groups. However, these groups can become crowded, making it difficult to find specific academic assistance.

Friends and Classmates: Students often depend on people they already know. This can limit their access to other students who may be better suited to help them.

Physical Study Groups: Students arrange physical meetings to study together. However, organizing these meetings can be difficult when students have different schedules.

EduMate intends to provide a more organized way for students to find academic partners and groups.

6. Proposed Solution

EduMate will provide a platform where students can create profiles, indicate the courses they offer or need help with, find other students and participate in study groups.

The platform will also support skill exchange, allowing students to share knowledge and learn from one another.

The main goal is to make academic collaboration easier and more accessible.

7. Project Objectives

The objectives of EduMate are to:

- Help students find suitable study partners.
- Make it easier to organize study groups.
- Encourage students to share academic knowledge.
- Create opportunities for students to teach and learn useful skills.
- Improve communication and collaboration among students.

8. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The first version of EduMate will focus on the basic features needed to solve the identified problem.

The MVP will include:

1. Student registration and login.
2. Student profile creation.
3. Course and skill selection.
4. Searching for other students.
5. Study group creation.
6. Basic skill exchange functionality.

Features such as video calls, payments and advanced recommendation systems will not be included in the first version.

9. Functional Requirements

The system should allow users to:

- Create an account and log in.
- Create and update their profiles.
- Add courses they are taking.
- Indicate subjects they need help with.
- List skills they can teach.
- Search for other students.
- Create and join study groups.
- View information about available study groups.

10. Non-Functional Requirements

Usability: The platform should be simple enough for students to use without special training.

Performance: Pages and basic operations should respond within a reasonable time.

Security: User information should be protected, and passwords should not be stored as plain text.

Reliability: The system should handle normal user activities without frequent errors.

Maintainability: The system should be organized so that future developers can update and improve it easily.

11. User Stories

- As a student, I want to create an account so that I can use EduMate.
- As a student, I want to add my courses so that other students can find me.
- As a student, I want to find people taking the same courses so that we can study together.
- As a student, I want to create a study group so that other interested students can join.
- As a student, I want to list my skills so that I can teach others.
- As a student, I want to find students with skills I want to learn.

12. System Workflow

The expected workflow is:

1. A student opens EduMate.
2. The student creates an account or logs in.
3. The student completes their profile.
4. The student adds their courses and skills.
5. The student searches for study partners or available groups.
6. The student selects a suitable student or joins a study group.
7. Students use the platform to support one another academically.

13. Data Requirements

The system will need to store information such as:

- Student ID.
- Full name.
- Email address.
- Department.
- Academic level.
- Courses.
- Skills.
- Study group information.
- Group membership information.

This information will help the platform organize students and connect them based on their academic interests.

14. Success Metrics

EduMate will be considered successful if:

- Students can register and create profiles successfully.
- Students can find others based on their courses.
- Students can create and join study groups.
- Students actively use the platform for academic collaboration.
- Users report that finding study partners has become easier.

15. Risks and Limitations

Some possible challenges include:

- Students may not be willing to create accounts.
- There may initially be a small number of registered users.
- Some students may provide incomplete or incorrect profile information.
- Students may join study groups but fail to participate.
- Maintaining accurate student information may be difficult.

These challenges can be addressed through proper user verification, simple registration and encouraging active participation.

16. Future Improvements

After the first version, possible improvements include:

- Private messaging between students.
- Notifications for study group activities.
- Timetable-based study scheduling.
- Better student matching.
- Academic resource sharing.
- More advanced skill exchange features.

17. Conclusion

EduMate is intended to make academic collaboration easier among Babcock University students. By helping students find study partners, organize study groups and exchange skills, the platform will provide another way for students to support one another outside the classroom.

The first version will focus on the essential features before additional improvements are introduced.
